title: The tangent normal's density
---

Let $\manifold$ be a space of dimension $\dimension$ with a reference measure $\nu$, let
$\mu \in \manifold$, let the chart at $\mu$ be global, with log-volume $\lambda_\mu$ and image
$V_\mu$, and let $W$ be a lower-triangular $\dimension \times \dimension$ matrix with a positive
diagonal. If $\zeta$ is a standard normal vector of $\R^\dimension$, then
$\randomPoint = \mu \oplus W^{-1} \zeta$ has, with respect to $\nu$, the density
$\exp \logterm_{\mathcal T}(\point;\, \mu, W)$ on $V_\mu$ and $0$ elsewhere. On a product of
Euclidean and positive factors with Lebesgue measure, $\logterm_{\mathcal T} = \logterm_{\mathcal N}$.
