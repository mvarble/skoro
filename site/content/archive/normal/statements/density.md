kind: proposition
title: The normal's density
---

Let $\manifold$ be a product of Euclidean and positive factors, of dimension $\dimension$, let
$\mu \in \manifold$, and let $W$ be a lower-triangular $\dimension \times \dimension$ matrix with a
positive diagonal. If $\zeta$ is a standard normal vector of $\R^\dimension$, then
$\randomPoint = \mu \oplus W^{-1} \zeta$ has, with respect to Lebesgue measure on $\manifold$, the
density $\exp \logterm_{\mathcal N}(\point;\, \mu, W)$, and its law is the normal distribution
$\mathcal N(\mu, \Sigma)$ on $\manifold$ with $\Sigma = W^{-1} W^{-\top}$.
