type: statement
kind: proposition
title: Linear-Gaussian terms
---

Let the free quantities of a query be of flat-in-disguise types, and let every term reached from
one of them bind a residual that is an affine function of their chart coordinates and a log-volume,
if any, that is affine in them. Then at every point $\hat \point$ the linearization is exact,
$m(\tangent) = f(\hat \point \oplus \tangent)$ for every $\tangent \in \R^\dimension$, and so is
the chart linearization.
