type: statement
kind: proposition
title: The linearized replacement
---

Let a retirement retire the quantities $E$, with $E_O$ given values and $E_F$ continuous, of types
with a volume and of total dimension $d_E$, and let $B_F$ be continuous quantities of its blanket
$B$, of total dimension $D$.
Let $m_T$ be the linearization at $\hat \point = (\hat \point_E, \hat \point_B)$ of the query of the
terms $\mathcal T_E$ alone, free in $E_F$ and $B_F$ and observing every other quantity, taken with
the chart's log-volume of $E_F$ alone, and let its rows have rank $d_E + D$, the number of their
columns. Then $e^{-m_T} = Z\, \mathcal N(\mu, \Sigma)$ on $\R^{d_E + D}$ for a normal density and a
constant $Z$, and replacing the integrand of the replacement $\logterm_B$ by $e^{-m_T}$ gives

$$
	\logterm_B(\hat \point_B \oplus \tangent_B) \approx \logterm_{\mathcal N}(\tangent_B;\, m, W) + \log Z ,
$$

with $m$ the mean and $W$ the lower-triangular whitening, with a positive diagonal, of the marginal
of $\mathcal N(\mu, \Sigma)$ on $\tangent_B$.
