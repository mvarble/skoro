---
type: statement
kind: proposition
title: The composite product retraction agrees with its factors
---

Let a product's retraction be built as the composite of slicing the point and the tangent into
blocks, applying each factor's retraction to its blocks, and concatenating the results. Then every
pass over a program containing it (values, tangent frames, cotangent frames, and Hessian products)
computes the same values as the factor-wise computation, block by block.
