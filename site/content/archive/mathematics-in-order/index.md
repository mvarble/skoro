The Mathematics section has so far been a collection of notes, one idea to a note, linked by what
each builds on: a reader could follow the links, but nothing told them where to begin or whether
they had seen it all. This entry makes the section the mathematical analogue of Concepts
(bottom-up), so that read from start to finish it tells the full story of all the mathematics on
the site, each note resting only on those before it.
