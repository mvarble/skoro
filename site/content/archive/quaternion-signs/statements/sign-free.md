type: statement
kind: proposition
title: The sign-free normal
---

Let $\mathcal N(\mu, \Sigma)$ be the normal on $S^3$, or on $S^3 \ltimes \R^3$ or
$S^3 \ltimes (\R^3 \times \R^3)$, with density $\density_\mu$ with respect to $\nu$. Write $-q$ for
the point whose quaternion is negated and whose other coordinates are kept. The *sign-free normal*
$\mathcal N^{\pm}(\mu, \Sigma)$ is the law of $b \zeta$, where $\zeta \sim \mathcal N(\mu,
\Sigma)$ and $b$ is $\pm 1$ with probability $\tfrac12$ each, independent of $\zeta$. Its
density with respect to $\nu$ is

$$
	\density^{\pm}_\mu(q) = \tfrac12 \bigl( \density_\mu(q) + \density_\mu(-q) \bigr) .
$$
