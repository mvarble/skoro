---
type: statement
kind: proposition
title: The charts of the rigid motions
---

On $S^3 \ltimes \R^3$, with the product $(q_1, p_1)(q_2, p_2) = (q_1 \otimes q_2,\ p_1 + \pi(q_1)
p_2)$, let

$$
	(q, p) \oplus (\phi, \rho) = \bigl(q \oplus \phi,\ p + \pi(q)\, \rho\bigr),
$$

and on $S^3 \ltimes (\R^3 \times \R^3)$, with $(q_1, v_1, p_1)(q_2, v_2, p_2) = (q_1 \otimes q_2,\
v_1 + \pi(q_1) v_2,\ p_1 + \pi(q_1) p_2)$, let

$$
	(q, v, p) \oplus (\phi, \eta, \rho) = \bigl(q \oplus \phi,\ v + \pi(q)\, \eta,\ p + \pi(q)\, \rho\bigr).
$$

Each is the left translation by the point of the chart at the identity, is global and full, has the
inverse $\bigl(\phi, \rho\bigr) = \bigl((q' \ominus q),\ \pi(q)\transpose (p' - p)\bigr)$ (and likewise
for $v$), and has, against the product $\nu \times$ Lebesgue, which is a Haar measure of the group,
the log-volume $\lambda(\phi, \cdot) = -3 \log(1 + \lVert \phi \rVert^2 / 16)$ of its rotation
alone.
