---
type: statement
kind: proposition
title: The derivatives in these charts are the exponential coordinates'
---

Let $\map$ be a smooth map between the types of this document and Euclidean spaces. Its derivative
in charts [](eq:math/derivatives-in-charts/derivative), read in the charts of this document, equals
its Jacobian read in exponential coordinates on the same side, $\point \Exp(\tangent)$. In
particular the derivative of $\Exp : \R^3 \to S^3$ at $\omega$ is the right Jacobian $J_r(\omega)$,
and that of $\Log$ at $q$ is $J_r(\Log q)^{-1}$. On the unit quaternions the chart and the
exponential agree to second order, $1 \oplus \tangent = \Exp(\tangent) + O(\lVert \tangent
\rVert^3)$, so the second derivatives of a map of unit quaternions at the centres of the charts agree
too; on the rigid motions they agree to first order only.
