---
type: statement
kind: proposition
title: A law on the unit quaternions as a law on the rotations
---

Let $\randomPoint$ be a random point of $S^3$ with density $\density$ with respect to $\nu$, and
$\nu_{SO(3)} = \tfrac12\, \pi_* \nu$. Then $\pi(\randomPoint)$ has, with respect to
$\nu_{SO(3)}$, the density $R \mapsto \density(q) + \density(-q)$, $q$ either preimage of $R$. If
$\randomPoint = \mu \oplus \varepsilon$ with $\varepsilon$ in $\R^3$, then $\randomPoint$ lies on
the far side of $\mu$, $\langle \randomPoint, \mu \rangle < 0$, exactly when
$\lvert \varepsilon \rvert > 4$; and the two points $\pm q$ over one rotation have chart coordinates
at $\mu$ whose lengths multiply to $16$.
