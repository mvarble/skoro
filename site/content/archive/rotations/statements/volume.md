type: statement
kind: proposition
title: The volume of the stereographic chart
---

Let $\nu$ be the Haar measure of $S^3$ normalized so that the chart of
[%full](statement:chart) has volume $1$ at its centre, which is $8$ times the surface measure of
$S^3 \subset \R^4$. The chart at every $q$ is global and full, and its log-volume is

$$
	\lambda_q(\tangent) = -3 \log\Bigl(1 + \tfrac{1}{16} \lvert \tangent \rvert^2\Bigr) .
$$
