type: statement
kind: proposition
title: The chart volumes of the core's types
---

With Lebesgue measure on $\R^n$, on $\R^{m \times n}$ and on $\positive^n$, the charts of the core's
Euclidean and positive types are global and full at every point, with the log-volumes

$$
	\lambda_\mu(\tangent) = 0 \ \text{on the Euclidean types}, \qquad
	\lambda_\mu(\tangent) = \sum_{i=1}^n \log \mu_i + \sum_{i=1}^n \tangent_i
	= \sum_{i=1}^n \log (\mu \oplus \tangent)_i \ \text{on} \ \positive^n .
$$

On a product of continuous types, with the product of the factors' measures, the chart at
$(\mu_1, \dots, \mu_k)$ has the log-volume $\sum_i \lambda_{\mu_i}(\tangent_i)$ at the stacked
tangent $(\tangent_1; \dots; \tangent_k)$, and is global, or full, if and only if every factor's is.
