`skoro-uniform` is skoro's [uniform distribution](../../../concepts/the-uniform/), the law
$\nu / \nu(\mathbb X)$ of a space whose reference measure has finite mass, built on the core's
public interface and on the residual role of [`skoro-normal`](../skoro-normal/). It takes the
categorical spaces, an embedded type that declares its mass, such as the rotations of
[`skoro-spatial`](../skoro-spatial/), and products of such embedded types, and refuses every other space with
the core's `NoMass`. It is how a model says that nothing is known of a quantity, which on a curved
type a normal of a large scale does not.

A `Uniform` is its space alone. `score` adds the constant term $-\log \nu(\mathbb X)$, a call of a
composite of the quantity that does not read it, so that its every derivative is zero and a
quantity of another space is refused by the core's check of an operation's inputs; gives it a
`UniformSample`, an exact sampler that draws on the host from the variates of each row's key, by
the type's declared draw or as $\lfloor n u \rfloor$ on a categorical space; and binds a residual of
no components, so that a linearization reads the term as a factor with no rows. A `Uniform` is a
`Distribution`, so the core's `sample` makes a quantity and scores it by one in a single call.
