title: Linear-Gaussian terms
---

Let $\point$ be a point of a space that is flat in disguise, and let every residual $r_t$ of
[](eq:objective) be an affine function of its chart coordinates, and every log-volume $\lambda_t$
affine in them. Then at every point $\hat \point$ the linearization is exact,
$m(\tangent) = f(\hat \point \oplus \tangent)$ for every $\tangent \in \R^\dimension$.
