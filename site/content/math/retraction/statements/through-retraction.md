kind: proposition
title: Derivatives through the retraction
---

For smooth $\map : \manifold \to \otherManifold$ and $\otherPoint = \map(\point)$, $\D\map(\point)$ is
the Jacobian at $0$ of $(\cdot \ominus \otherPoint) \circ \map \circ (\point \oplus \cdot)$, a map
between neighbourhoods of $0$ in Euclidean spaces.
