title: Retraction
---

A _retraction_ on a smooth manifold $\manifold$ is a smooth map $\retr : \T\manifold \to \manifold$ such that, for every $\point \in \manifold$, its restriction $\retr_\point$ to $\T_\point\manifold$ satisfies

1. $\retr_\point(0_\point) = \point$, and
2. $\dd(\retr_\point)_{0_\point} = \mathrm{id}_{\T_\point\manifold}$, where $\T_{0_\point}(\T_\point\manifold)$ is identified with $\T_\point\manifold$ canonically.
