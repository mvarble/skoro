title: The iteration of any curvature
---

Let $M = \sum_t M_t$ be invertible near the iterates of

$$
	q_{k+1} = q_k \oplus \delta_k, \qquad M(q_k)\, \delta_k = -g(q_k) ,
$$

the minimizer of the quadratic model [](eq:model) at each $q_k$. Then $\delta_k = 0$ if and only if
$q_k$ is a stationary point of $f$. If $q^\ast$ is a stationary point, $H^\ast$ the chart Hessian of
$f$ there, and the spectral radius $\rho$ of $M^{-1} (H^\ast - M)$ at $q^\ast$ is less than $1$,
the iteration converges to $q^\ast$ from every start near enough to it, at the linear rate $\rho$.
