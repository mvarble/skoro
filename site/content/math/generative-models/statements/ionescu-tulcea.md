type: statement
kind: theorem
title: Ionescu-Tulcea
---

Let $(\manifold_i, \mathscr X_i)_{i \ge 1}$ be any measurable spaces, $\mu_1$ a probability measure
on $\manifold_1$, and for each $i \ge 2$ let $K_i$ be a probability kernel from
$\manifold_1 \times \cdots \times \manifold_{i-1}$ to $\manifold_i$. There is a unique probability
measure $\mu$ on $\prod_{i \ge 1} \manifold_i$, with its product $\sigma$-algebra, whose marginal on
every finite initial product is $\mu_1 \otimes K_2 \otimes \cdots \otimes K_i$.
