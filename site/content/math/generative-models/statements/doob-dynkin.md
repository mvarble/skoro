type: statement
kind: lemma
title: Doob–Dynkin
---

Let $\randomPoint : \Omega \to \manifold$ be a measurable map from a measurable space $(\Omega, \mathscr F)$ to
any measurable space $(\manifold, \mathscr X)$, and $\otherRandomPoint : \Omega \to \R$. Then $\otherRandomPoint$ is
$\sigma(\randomPoint)$-measurable if and only if $\otherRandomPoint = g \circ \randomPoint$ for some measurable $g : \manifold \to \R$.
