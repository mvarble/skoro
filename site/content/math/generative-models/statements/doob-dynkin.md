type: statement
kind: lemma
title: Doob–Dynkin
---

Let $\bfx : \Omega \to \manifold$ be a measurable map from a measurable space $(\Omega, \mathscr F)$ to
any measurable space $(\manifold, \mathscr X)$, and $\bfy : \Omega \to \R$. Then $\bfy$ is
$\sigma(\bfx)$-measurable if and only if $\bfy = g \circ \bfx$ for some measurable $g : \manifold \to \R$.
