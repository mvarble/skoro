---
type: statement
kind: lemma
title: Doob–Dynkin
---

Let $X : \Omega \to \manifold$ be a measurable map from a measurable space $(\Omega, \mathscr F)$ to
any measurable space $(\manifold, \mathscr X)$, and $Y : \Omega \to \R$. Then $Y$ is
$\sigma(X)$-measurable if and only if $Y = g \circ X$ for some measurable $g : \manifold \to \R$.
