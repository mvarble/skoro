---
type: statement
kind: proposition
title: A normal with a large scale
---

Let $\manifold$ be a manifold with a retraction whose chart at $\mu$ is global, with image $V_\mu$, and let $\randomPoint_k = \mu \oplus \varepsilon_k$, where $\varepsilon_k$ is normal on $\R^\dimension$ with mean zero and covariance $\Sigma_k$, and $\det \Sigma_k \to \infty$.
Then

$$
	\Pr(\randomPoint_k \in K) \to 0
$$

for every compact $K \subseteq V_\mu$.
If moreover $\manifold$ is compact and the chart is full, the total variation distance between the law of $\randomPoint_k$ and the uniform distribution tends to $1$, and every weak limit of the laws of $\randomPoint_k$ is supported on $\manifold \setminus V_\mu$, a set of measure zero.
