type: statement
kind: proposition
title: The uniform distribution in a chart
---

Let $\manifold$ be a manifold with a retraction and a finite reference measure $\nu$, and let the
chart at $\mu$ be full, with domain $U_\mu$ and volume $v_\mu$. If $\varepsilon$ is a random vector
of $\R^\dimension$ with the density

$$
	f(\tangent) = \frac{v_\mu(\tangent)}{\nu(\manifold)}
$$

on $U_\mu$, and zero outside it, then $\mu \oplus \varepsilon$ is uniform on $\manifold$. Conversely,
if $\randomPoint$ is uniform on $\manifold$, then $\randomPoint \ominus \mu$, defined almost surely,
has the density $f$.
