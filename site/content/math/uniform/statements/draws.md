---
type: statement
kind: proposition
title: Uniform points from uniform variates
---

Let $u_1, u_2, u_3$ be independent and uniform on $(0, 1)$.

1. With $c = 1 - 2 u_1$ and $\varphi = 2 \pi u_2$, the point

    $$
    	\bigl(\sqrt{1 - c^2} \cos \varphi,\ \sqrt{1 - c^2} \sin \varphi,\ c\bigr)
    $$

    is uniform on $S^2$.

2. With $\varphi = 2 \pi u_2$ and $\psi = 2 \pi u_3$, the point

    $$
    	\bigl(\sqrt{1 - u_1} \cos \varphi,\ \sqrt{1 - u_1} \sin \varphi,\ \sqrt{u_1} \cos \psi,\ \sqrt{u_1} \sin \psi\bigr)
    $$

    is uniform on $S^3$, and the rotation it is a unit quaternion of is uniform on $SO(3)$.
