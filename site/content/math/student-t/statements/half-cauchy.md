---
type: statement
kind: proposition
title: The half-Cauchy law
---

Let $s > 0$ and $u$ be uniform on $(0, 1)$. Then $\tau = s \tan(\pi u / 2)$ has the law of
$\lvert \varepsilon \rvert$ for $\varepsilon$ a Cauchy variable of scale $s$, with the density

$$
	\logterm_{C^+}(\tau;\, s) = \log \frac{2}{\pi} - \log s - \log\Bigl(1 + \frac{\tau^2}{s^2}\Bigr) @tag(half-cauchy)
$$

on $\positive$ with respect to Lebesgue measure.
