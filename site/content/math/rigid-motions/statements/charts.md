type: statement
kind: proposition
title: The charts of the rigid motions
---

Let $q \oplus \phi$ be a retraction of the rotations that is the left translation of its chart at
the identity, $q \oplus \phi = q \otimes (1 \oplus \phi)$, with inverse $q' \ominus q$. On the rigid
motions let

$$
	(q, p) \oplus (\phi, \rho) = \bigl(q \oplus \phi,\ p + \pi(q)\, \rho\bigr),
$$

and on the extended poses let

$$
	(q, v, p) \oplus (\phi, \eta, \rho) = \bigl(q \oplus \phi,\ v + \pi(q)\, \eta,\ p + \pi(q)\, \rho\bigr).
$$

Each is a retraction, the left translation by the point of the chart at the identity, with the
inverse

$$
	(q', p') \ominus (q, p) = \bigl(q' \ominus q,\ \pi(q)\transpose (p' - p)\bigr)
$$

and likewise for $v$, defined wherever the rotations' inverse is. Its domain is the rotation
chart's domain times the whole of the translations' coordinates, and with the Cayley chart of the
rotations it is unchanged by the sign of either quaternion.
