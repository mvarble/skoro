---
type: statement
kind: proposition
title: The product retraction
---

If each $R^i$ is a retraction on $\mathcal M_i$, then

$$
	R_x(\delta) = \bigl(R^1_{x_1}(\delta_1), \dots, R^k_{x_k}(\delta_k)\bigr)
$$

is a retraction on $\mathcal M = \mathcal M_1 \times \cdots \times \mathcal M_k$, its inverse is
$y \ominus x = (y_1 \ominus x_1; \dots; y_k \ominus x_k)$, and it is flat when every factor is.
