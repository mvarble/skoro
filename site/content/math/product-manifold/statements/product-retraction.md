---
type: statement
kind: proposition
title: The product retraction
---

If each $R^i$ is a retraction on $\manifold_i$, then

$$
	R_\point(\tangent) = \bigl(R^1_{\point_1}(\tangent_1), \dots, R^k_{\point_k}(\tangent_k)\bigr)
$$

is a retraction on $\manifold = \manifold_1 \times \cdots \times \manifold_k$, its inverse is
$\otherPoint \ominus \point = (\otherPoint_1 \ominus \point_1; \dots; \otherPoint_k \ominus \point_k)$, and it is flat when every factor is.
