---
type: statement
kind: proposition
title: The antipode in the stereographic chart
---

Let $\mu, q \in S^3$ with $q \neq \pm\mu$, and let $\tangent = q \ominus \mu$ in the stereographic
chart. Then

$$
	(-q) \ominus \mu = q \ominus (-\mu) = -\frac{16}{\lVert \tangent \rVert^2}\, \tangent ,
$$

and $\lVert \tangent \rVert < 4$ exactly when $\langle q, \mu \rangle > 0$. Of the two points
$\pm q$ over one rotation, the one on the side of $\mu$ has the coordinate of length
$4 \tan(\theta / 4) \le 4$, with $\theta \in [0, \pi]$ the angle between the rotations $\pi(q)$ and
$\pi(\mu)$, and the other the coordinate of length $4 \cot(\theta / 4) \ge 4$ in the opposite
direction. The two lengths multiply to $16$.
