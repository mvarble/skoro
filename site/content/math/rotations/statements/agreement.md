type: statement
kind: proposition
title: The charts agree with the exponential to second order
---

In the stereographic chart and in the Cayley chart alike, $1 \oplus \tangent = \Exp(\tangent) +
O(\lVert \tangent \rVert^3)$, and so $q \oplus \tangent = q \otimes \Exp(\tangent) + O(\lVert
\tangent \rVert^3)$ at every $q$. Hence the derivative in charts of a smooth map between rotations
and Euclidean spaces is its Jacobian in exponential coordinates on the right, $q \otimes
\Exp(\tangent)$, and the second derivatives of the map at the centres of the charts are those in
exponential coordinates too. In particular the derivative of $\Exp : \R^3 \to S^3$ at $\omega$ is
the right Jacobian $J_r(\omega)$, and that of $\Log$ at $q$ is $J_r(\Log q)^{-1}$.
