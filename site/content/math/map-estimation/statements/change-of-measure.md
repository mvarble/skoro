---
type: statement
kind: proposition
title: A change of measure moves the mode
---

Let $\nu_F' = h\, \nu_F$ for a positive measurable function $h$ of the free quantities. The
posterior is the same law against either measure, its density against $\nu_F'$ is its density
against $\nu_F$ divided by $h$, and the objective against $\nu_F'$ is

$$
	f'(\point_F) = f(\point_F) + \log h(\point_F) .
$$

The two objectives differ by $\log h$: when $h$ is constant they have the same minimizers, and
when it is not they have, in general, different ones.
