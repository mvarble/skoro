type: statement
kind: proposition
title: The volume of a product chart
---

For $i = 1, \dots, k$ let $\manifold_i$ be a manifold of dimension $\dimension_i$ with a retraction
and a reference measure $\nu_i$, and let the chart at $\mu_i$ have domain $U_i$, image $V_i$ and
volume $v_i$. Give $\manifold = \manifold_1 \times \cdots \times \manifold_k$ the product measure
$\nu = \nu_1 \otimes \cdots \otimes \nu_k$ and the product retraction. The chart at
$\mu = (\mu_1, \dots, \mu_k)$ has domain $U = U_1 \times \cdots \times U_k$, image
$V = V_1 \times \cdots \times V_k$ and, at the stacked tangent $\tangent = (\tangent_1; \dots;
\tangent_k)$,

$$
	v_\mu(\tangent) = \prod_{i=1}^k v_i(\tangent_i), \qquad
	\lambda_\mu(\tangent) = \sum_{i=1}^k \lambda_i(\tangent_i) .
$$

It is global if and only if every factor's chart is, and full if and only if every factor's chart
is. If every $\nu_i$ is the measure $\manifold_i$'s retraction normalizes, $\nu$ is the measure the
product retraction normalizes.
