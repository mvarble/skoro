type: statement
kind: proposition
title: The measure a retraction normalizes
---

Let $\manifold$ be a manifold of dimension $\dimension$ with a retraction whose identification of
each tangent space with $\R^\dimension$ is by a basis $b(\point) = \D R_\point(0)$, smooth in
$\point$. There is exactly one measure $\nu$ on $\manifold$ with a continuous positive density in
every chart such that every chart has volume $v_\mu(0) = 1$ at its centre: the Riemannian volume
measure of the metric

$$
	g_\point\bigl(b(\point)\, u,\ b(\point)\, w\bigr) = u\transpose w ,
$$

in which every basis $b(\point)$ is orthonormal. It depends on the bases alone: two retractions with
the same bases normalize the same measure, whatever their behaviour beyond first order and however
the manifold is stored.
