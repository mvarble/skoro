type: statement
kind: proposition
title: The measure a retraction normalizes
---

Let $\manifold$ be a manifold of dimension $\dimension$ with a retraction $\oplus$ smooth on an open $W \subseteq \manifold$.
There is exactly one measure $\nu$ on $W$ with a continuous positive density in every chart such that every chart centred in $W$ has volume $v_\mu(0) = 1$ at its centre: the Riemannian volume measure of the metric

$$
	g_\point(u, w) = u\transpose w , \qquad u, w \in \T_\point\manifold ,
$$

in which the tangent coordinates are orthonormal.
It depends on the tangent coordinates alone: two retractions whose curves $t \mapsto \point \oplus t\tangent$ have the same velocities at $t = 0$ normalize the same measure, whatever their behaviour beyond first order and however the manifold is stored.
