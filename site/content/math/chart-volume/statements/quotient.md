type: statement
kind: proposition
title: Densities and charts on a quotient
---

Let a finite group $G$ of $m$ elements act freely on $\manifold$ by diffeomorphisms that preserve
the reference measure $\nu$, with quotient $N$ and quotient map $\varpi$, and give $N$ the measure
$\nu_N = \tfrac1m\, \varpi_* \nu$.

1. If a random point $\randomPoint$ of $\manifold$ has the density $\density$ with respect to
   $\nu$, then $\varpi(\randomPoint)$ has, with respect to $\nu_N$, the density $[\point] \mapsto
   \sum_{g \in G} \density(g \point)$.
2. Let the retraction of $\manifold$ descend to $N$, and let its chart at $\mu$ be a diffeomorphism
   of $U$ onto a set $V$ on which $\varpi$ is injective. Then the chart of $N$ at $[\mu]$ has, on
   $U$ and with respect to $\nu_N$, the volume $v_\mu$ of the chart of $\manifold$, the same at
   every representative. If $\nu$ is the measure the retraction of $\manifold$ normalizes, $\nu_N$
   is the measure the retraction of $N$ normalizes.
