type: statement

title: A density pushed through a local chart
---

With the chart of [%full](statement:chart-volume), let $R_\mu$ be defined on all of $\R^\dimension$,
let $\varepsilon$ have density $f$ on $\R^\dimension$, and let $p = \Pr(\varepsilon \notin U_\mu) < 1$.
Let $P$ be the law of $\mu \oplus \varepsilon$, and $Q$ the law with density
$\density / (1 - p)$ with respect to $\nu$, $\density$ as in [%full](statement:pushforward). Then
$\density$ integrates to $1 - p$, $Q$ is the law of $\mu \oplus \varepsilon$ given
$\varepsilon \in U_\mu$, and

$$
	\sup_S \, \lvert P(S) - Q(S) \rvert \le p ,
$$

the supremum taken over the measurable sets $S$ of $\manifold$.
