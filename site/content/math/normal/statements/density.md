title: The normal's density
---

Let $\manifold$ be a manifold of dimension $\dimension$ with a retraction and a reference measure
$\nu$, let $\mu \in \manifold$, let the chart at $\mu$ be global, with log-volume $\lambda_\mu$ and
image $V_\mu$, and let $W$ be a lower-triangular $\dimension \times \dimension$ matrix with a
positive diagonal. If $\zeta$ is a standard normal vector of $\R^\dimension$, then
$\randomPoint = \mu \oplus W^{-1} \zeta$ has, with respect to $\nu$, the density
$\exp \logterm_{\mathcal N}(\point;\, \mu, W)$ on $V_\mu$ and $0$ elsewhere, and its law is the
normal distribution $\mathcal N(\mu, \Sigma)$ on $\manifold$ with $\Sigma = W^{-1} W^{-\top}$.
