title: The normalizer of a translated chart
---

Let $G$ be a Lie group with a Haar measure $\nu$ and the retraction $R_\mu(\tangent) = \mu\,
\varphi(\tangent)$ of [%full](statement:math/chart-volume/group), whose chart is full, and let $W$
be fixed. Then

$$
	Z(\mu) = \int_{V_\mu} \exp\bigl(-\tfrac12 \lVert W (z \ominus \mu) \rVert^2\bigr) \, \dd \nu(z)
	= \int_U \exp\bigl(-\tfrac12 \lVert W \tangent \rVert^2\bigr)\, v_e(\tangent) \, \dd \tangent
$$

does not depend on $\mu$.
