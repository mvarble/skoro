---
title: Retractions
summary: Maps from a tangent space back onto the manifold.
depends_on: [tangent-space]
---

A retraction $R_x : T_x\mathcal{M} \to \mathcal{M}$ satisfies

$$
	R_x(0) = x, \qquad \mathrm{D}R_x(0) = \mathrm{id} @tag(fixed)
$$

By [](eq:fixed), it agrees with the exponential map to first order [](cite:folland1999).

## In skoro

Each manifold type offers one; see [the Normal](../../archive/normal/math.svx).
