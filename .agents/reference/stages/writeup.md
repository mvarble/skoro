# skoro's writeup

What `/wf-writeup` asks of skoro besides what the skill says.

A decision is a position the library takes: what it is for, how its core is shaped, what it will
not do. When working one out, ask what the user built before: the `archive` branch is the record of
skoro before its restart (`AGENTS.md`, under "The archive branch"). The decision is argued at the
level of the library's interfaces, with code only to show those. A long decision (the library's
charter, say) may use its own sections. After a reopening, keep a `## Revisions` section as
`/wf-math` does.
